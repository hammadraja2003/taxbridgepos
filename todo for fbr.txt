Suppose:
MRP = 1000
Customer A ko sale = 1000
Customer B ko sale = 900
Aapka sawal:
Kya dono cases me sales tax 1000 par lagega?
Correct Answer
Haan — agar product Third Schedule me hai
To:
Case	              Selling Price	            Sales Tax Base
Customer A	          1000	                    1000
Customer B	          900	                    1000
Sales tax MRP par hi calculate hoga, chahe discount de diya ho.

sales tax MRP pr calculate hoga lakin discount sale price pr calculate hoga like MRP 1000 ha to sales tax 1000 pr calculate hoga lakin age ham customer ko  wo prodcut 900 ki dety hain to total value excluding tax 900 show hogi  aur 1000 pr  18 % calculate ho kar total value including tax show hogi jo k 1080 show ho rhi ha 

hamary sytem me sales tax to dono ka MRP pr calculate ho rha ha but Total Value Excluding Tax and Total Value Including Tax ko review karna pary ga
Rule (Third Schedule Product ke liye)

Sales Tax hamesha MRP par calculate hota hai
Discount / Sale price change ho sakti hai
Tax amount fix rehta hai (jab tak MRP same hai)
Case 1
MRP = 1000
Tax rate = 18%
➡ Sales Tax = 1000 × 18% = 180
Agar product 900 me sell ki:
Value (Excl. Tax) = 900
Sales Tax = 180
Total (Incl. Tax) = 1080
Correct
Case 2
Same product, MRP abhi bhi 1000
Agar product 950 me sell ki:
Value (Excl. Tax) = 950
Sales Tax = 180 (same rahega)
Total (Incl. Tax) = 1130
Correct

Third Schedule Product kya hota hai?

Pakistan me sales tax law ke andar kuch products “Third Schedule” me hote hain.
Ye aam products jaise nahi hote.
In products par:
Retail price (MRP) par tax lagta hai
Invoice me special rules follow karne hote hain
FBR zyada strict monitoring karti hai
Example (samajhne ke liye):
Mobile phones
Electronics (kuch categories)
Cigarettes, etc.
